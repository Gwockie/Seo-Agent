# Psychology guidance

Load only for a saved audit selecting psychology. Take services, audience,
geography, brand aliases and intended landing pages from that site's saved
configuration. An industry intent definition (ADHD assessment/testing/evaluation,
psychological testing, therapy) is vocabulary, not proof that a practice offers it.

Use only that site's confirmed facts. Never invent licensure, qualifications,
expertise, outcomes, diagnoses, instruments, insurance, affiliations, reviews or
testimonials. Mark unconfirmed factual items for clinician confirmation. Near-me
queries express local intent; they do not require literal repetition in copy.

Indexing/canonical/content leads need that site's own crawl/inspection/query rows.
Do not transfer the original practice's Paoli goals or October 6 observation to
another client. Reported fixes remain user-reported until dated implementation
evidence and fresh inspection support verification. Never invent their actions or
claim causality from before/after performance.
